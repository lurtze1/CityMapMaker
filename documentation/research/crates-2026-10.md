# Crate survey, October 2026

> Written 2026-10-19 by a research agent. A claim here is binding only when a DECISIONS.md
> entry cites it. Versions and dates are snapshots; re-check when the ADR is written.

Researched 2026-10-19; versions and dates are snapshots and must be re-checked when the ADR is written.

Scope: the crates named in the open decisions of `documentation/DECISIONS.md` (geometry at milestone 0,
YAML at milestone 1, font metrics at milestone 7) plus the supporting crates whose roles
ARCHITECTURE.md describes without naming a crate. Constraints applied:
MIT OR Apache-2.0 compatible license, builds for `wasm32-unknown-unknown` when it is a candidate
for `citymap-core`, Windows MSVC primary, CI on Windows and Linux.

How the facts were obtained (so you know what "verified" means here):

- Version, release date, license, repository: crates.io API (`/api/v1/crates/<name>`).
- Last push and open-issue count: GitHub API (`/repos/<owner>/<repo>`); the count includes open PRs.
- **wasm32**: a throwaway crate in the session scratchpad depending on all candidates was
  `cargo check --target wasm32-unknown-unknown`-ed per package with the project's pinned
  toolchain (rustc 1.99.1), default features. "OK" below means it compiled; tools and CLI-only
  crates were not checked and are marked "n/a".
- **unsafe**: the downloaded sources in `~/.cargo/registry` were grepped for `unsafe` blocks and
  for `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`. Numbers are counts of lines containing `unsafe` in `src/`, so a
  small number can include comments; "forbid" means the attribute is in `lib.rs`.
- Anything not obtained one of those ways is labelled "unverified".

## Per-crate notes

### geo
- https://crates.io/crates/geo, https://github.com/georust/geo, https://docs.rs/geo
- 0.33.1, 2026-04-20; MIT OR Apache-2.0; edition 2024, MSRV 1.88; pushed 2026-10-18.
- Direct deps as resolved: `earcut =0.4.5`, `geo-types 0.7.20`, `i_overlay 4.5.2` (constraint `>=4.5.1, <4.6.0`), `rstar 0.12.2`, `spade 2.15.1`, `robust 1.2`, `rand 0.10`, `rand_pcg 0.10`, `geographiclib-rs`, `sif-itree`, `float_next_after`, `log`, `num-traits`.
- Breaking-change history: 0.31 (2025-09) `BoolOpsNum: GeoFloat`, i_overlay 4.0; 0.32 (2025-12) `concave_hull` signature, feature renames; 0.33 (2026-04) earcut output order, `earcutr` replaced by `earcut`, i_overlay 4.4 for OGC-valid boolean output. Breaking minors so far: 2025-09, 2025-12, 2026-04, which is one every three to four months.
- The one thing to know: **`geo` locks `i_overlay` to 4.5.x and `rstar` to 0.12** while the current majors are 9 and 0.13. Depending on both `geo` and a newer `i_overlay`/`rstar` directly gives two copies in the tree (verified in the scratch build) and type mismatches at the API boundary. Either go through `geo` for booleans/buffer/R-tree, or skip `geo` and use `geo-types` plus the newer crates directly.
- Unsafe: two lines (`get_unchecked` in a windows iterator; a `transmute` in `old_sweep`). Not `forbid`.

### geo-types
- https://crates.io/crates/geo-types, https://docs.rs/geo-types
- 0.7.20, 2026-08-02; MIT OR Apache-2.0; no_std; deps `num-traits`, `thiserror 2`, optional `serde`, `approx`, `rstar` (one feature per rstar major: `rstar_0_8` … `rstar_0_13`), `spade`.
- The one thing to know: enable exactly one `rstar_0_NN` feature and keep it equal to the `rstar` major you use. 0.7.x has been additive since 2021; `Coord` field access and `coord!` macro are the stable surface.

### spade
- https://crates.io/crates/spade, https://github.com/Stoeoef/spade, https://docs.rs/spade
- 2.15.1, 2026-03-24; MIT OR Apache-2.0; `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`; no_std (optional `std`); deps `robust`, `hashbrown`, `smallvec`, `num-traits`.
- Delaunay, constrained Delaunay, refinement, Voronoi extraction, bulk loading, vertex removal; exact predicates via `robust` by default.
- Degenerate input: `add_constraint` **panics** if the new constraint crosses an existing one; use `try_add_constraint` (returns empty Vec, no change) or `add_constraint_and_split`; `bulk_load_cdt` dedupes equal positions to the lowest index and panics on crossing constraints, `try_bulk_load_cdt` (2.15.0) reports them via callback. `locate*` panics on NaN since 2.12.0. 2.15.1 fixed an endless loop in refinement.
- The one thing to know: constraint edges may touch at endpoints but never cross; the generator must pre-split road segments at intersections before building the CDT, or use the `_and_split` variants and accept new vertices. Roadmap mentions a Spade 3 that may drop non-f64 outputs.

### i_overlay
- https://crates.io/crates/i_overlay, https://github.com/iShape-Rust/iOverlay, https://docs.rs/i_overlay
- 9.1.0, 2026-10-14; MIT OR Apache-2.0; edition 2024, MSRV 1.88; no_std; deps `i_float`, `i_shape`, `i_tree`, `i_key_sort`, optional `rayon`.
- Boolean ops (union, difference, intersection, xor), self-intersection resolution, all four fill rules, OGC-valid output option, offset/buffer, simplify, polyline slicing and clipping.
- Works on an integer grid: float input is snapped (`i32` default engine, `i64` available, fixed-scale variants return errors instead of panicking). Finite input up to 2^500 for f64; infallible APIs panic on invalid bounds. Offsetting is only reliable on valid, correctly oriented polygons; simplify first.
- Unsafe: 49 lines in `i_overlay`, plus 19 in `i_tree` and 17 in `i_key_sort` (index-based performance code). Not `forbid`.
- The one thing to know: **major version every one to three months** (2.0 Feb 2025, 3.0 Apr 2025, 4.0 May 2025, 5.0 Apr 2026, 6.0 May 2026, 7.0 Jun 2026, 8.0 Aug 2026, 9.0 Sep 2026) and no changelog or GitHub releases. Integer snapping is good for determinism but means output vertices are quantised; pick the grid once and record it.

### rstar
- https://crates.io/crates/rstar, https://github.com/georust/rstar, https://docs.rs/rstar
- 0.13.0, 2026-05-24; MIT OR Apache-2.0; `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`; no_std + alloc; deps `heapless`, `smallvec`, `num-traits`, optional `serde`, `mint`.
- The one thing to know: `geo` 0.33 still uses 0.12 (0.13 only under "Unreleased" in geo's changelog). Bulk loading is deterministic for a given insertion order; always insert in id order.

### libm
- https://crates.io/crates/libm, https://github.com/rust-lang/compiler-builtins (directory `libm/`), https://docs.rs/libm
- 0.2.16, 2026-01-24 on crates.io (changelog header says 2025-12-07); MIT; MSRV 1.63; no deps; no_std.
- Unsafe: 56 lines, arch-specific intrinsics and inline assembly behind the default `arch` feature (x86 FMA runtime detection since 0.2.14, i586 assembly for `exp`/`floor`/`ceil` in 0.2.16).
- The one thing to know: this is the cross-platform determinism anchor (CLAUDE.md rule 5), so **pin `=0.2.16`** and note that the `arch` feature selects different code paths per CPU; disable it (`default-features = false`) if byte-identical results across x86 and wasm matter more than speed. The changelog lists no accuracy changes to `sin`/`cos`/`atan2`/`sqrt`/`pow` in 0.2.12–0.2.16, but `cbrt` was reimplemented in 0.2.12.

### rand_pcg and rand_core
- https://crates.io/crates/rand_pcg (0.10.2, 2026-04-11), https://crates.io/crates/rand_core (0.10.1, 2026-04-13); both MIT OR Apache-2.0; both no_std; `rand_pcg` is `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`, `rand_core` has no unsafe blocks.
- The one thing to know: `rand` 0.10.3 is current; `rand_core` 0.9 → 0.10 renamed trait items, and the tree still contains `rand_core 0.6` via `noise`'s old `rand 0.8`. `Pcg64Mcg` output is algorithmically fixed, which is why ADR-018 picks it; pin the exact version anyway.

### serde
- https://crates.io/crates/serde, https://github.com/serde-rs/serde; 1.0.229, 2026-07-18; MIT OR Apache-2.0; split into `serde_core` + `serde` since 1.0.220; two unsafe lines each. Nothing to know beyond "use `derive`".

#### serde-saphyr
- https://crates.io/crates/serde-saphyr, https://github.com/bourumir-wyngs/serde-saphyr, https://docs.rs/serde-saphyr
- 2.0.0, 2026-10-13; MIT OR Apache-2.0; `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` (backend `granit-parser` 2.0 also forbid); edition 2024, MSRV 1.89; wasm32-unknown-unknown and wasip1 in upstream CI, confirmed here.
- Deserialise and serialise (`to_string`, `serialize` feature on by default); no `Value` DOM (use `serde_json::Value` if needed); duplicate keys are an **error by default** (good for specs); anchors, aliases, merge keys (policy configurable); fuzz-tested, documented panic-free on malformed input; error messages with location via `annotate-snippets`.
- 2.0.0 breaking changes: `from_multiple*` now take `Deserialize<'de>`; `Error::AliasError` gained a mandatory field. Small.
- Deps: `annotate-snippets`, `encoding_rs_io`, `granit-parser`, `num-traits`, `smallvec`, `base64`, `nohash-hasher`, `zmij`, `serde_core`; optional `figment`, `garde`, `miette`, `validator`.
- The one thing to know: 2.0.0 is six days old and the crate is one person's project (plus the earlier `serde_yaml_bw` 2.5.8 by the same author, which it supersedes). Pin the minor and keep `serde_path_to_error` in front of it; whether saphyr's own error paths make `serde_path_to_error` redundant is unverified.

### serde_path_to_error

### schemars

### resvg, usvg, tiny-skia
- https://crates.io/crates/resvg, https://github.com/linebender/resvg (moved to Linebender in 0.45, 2025-02); 0.48.1, 2026-08-02; Apache-2.0 OR MIT; both `resvg` and `usvg` are `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`; MSRV 1.85 (lowered from 1.89 in 0.48.1); compile for wasm (confirmed; system font loading is the documented rough edge).
- `tiny-skia` 0.12.0, 2026-02-02, **BSD-3-Clause** (permissive; add to the cargo-deny allow list); 151 unsafe lines for SIMD and `bytemuck` casts, documented as "fully safe in practice".
- Breaking-change cadence: every 0.x minor (0.46 edition 2024 + kurbo 0.13; 0.47 tiny-skia 0.12 + variable fonts; 0.48 **text moved from ttf-parser/rustybuzz to skrifa/harfrust**, about 500 KB larger, small rendering differences).
- The one thing to know: text in the rendered PNG is shaped by `skrifa` + `harfrust` since 0.48. If core measures labels with a different font stack (ttf-parser), measured and rendered widths can differ slightly; measure with `skrifa` to keep one stack, or render labels as outlines produced in core.

### ttf-parser
- https://crates.io/crates/ttf-parser, https://github.com/harfbuzz/ttf-parser; 0.25.1, 2024-11-29; MIT OR Apache-2.0; `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`, zero heap allocation, no_std, MSRV 1.63; optional `core_maths` for no_std floats. Pushed 2026-08-06, 5 open.
- Provides advances, side bearings, glyph outlines (glyf/CFF/CFF2, variable), `kern`, GPOS/GSUB tables (`opentype-layout` feature), cmap.
- The one thing to know: the README now states **maintenance mode, bug fixes only, and recommends fontations (`read-fonts`, `skrifa`) for new projects**. Still the smallest, safest, most deterministic choice for "sum of advance widths".

### fontdue
- https://crates.io/crates/fontdue, https://github.com/mooman219/fontdue; 0.9.5 released 2026-10-19 (today); MIT OR Apache-2.0 OR Zlib; wraps `ttf-parser 0.25` and adds a rasteriser and a simple layout engine; `hashbrown` dep; 30 unsafe lines (0.9.4 fixed a soundness issue, #172).
- The one thing to know: it is a CPU rasteriser; this project rasterises through resvg, so fontdue would add a second rasteriser and unsafe code for no metric it does not already get from ttf-parser.

### skrifa (added: successor named by ttf-parser and used by usvg)
- https://crates.io/crates/skrifa, https://github.com/googlefonts/fontations, https://docs.rs/skrifa
- 0.48.0, 2026-10-03; MIT OR Apache-2.0; `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`; no_std with optional `libm`; deps `read-fonts 0.45`, `bytemuck`, optional `core_maths`; Google Fonts team, pushed 2026-10-19, 316 open issues across the mono-repo.
- Global and glyph metrics with variations, cmap, outlines with hinting, COLR. Documented goal: no panics on corrupt fonts.
- Unverified: wasm32 build (not in the scratch crate); release cadence (0.x, version numbers move fast: 0.48 in Oct 2026).
- The one thing to know: it is what resvg 0.48 uses for text, so adopting it keeps one font stack across core and render.

### clap, thiserror, anyhow, tracing, tracing-subscriber

### insta, cargo-insta, proptest, cargo-deny
- insta / cargo-insta 1.49.0 (2026-10-03, **Apache-2.0 only**, fine for a dev-dependency and tool); proptest 1.12.0 (2026-10-10, MIT OR Apache-2.0, MSRV 1.88, now on rand 0.10); cargo-deny 0.20.2 (2026-07-09, MIT OR Apache-2.0).
- The one thing to know: `insta` snapshot formatting changed between 1.3x releases in the past; pin the minor in `Cargo.lock` and run `cargo insta review`, never `--accept` blindly (CLAUDE.md already says so). cargo-deny needs explicit allows for BSD-3-Clause, ISC, Zlib, BSL-1.0 and (likely, unverified) Unicode-3.0 from transitive crates.

### delaunator
- https://crates.io/crates/delaunator, https://github.com/mourner/delaunator-rs; 1.1.0, 2026-05-12; ISC; no unsafe found; MSRV 1.87; dep `robust`.
- Collinear input returns an empty triangulation with all points on the hull; near-duplicates within an undocumented EPSILON are dropped.
- The one thing to know: no constraints, no Voronoi, no removal; it only wins on raw speed, which the city sizes here do not need. Rejected in favour of spade.

### earcut and earcutr
- `earcutr` 0.5.0 (2025-05-29, ISC) **archived 2026-05-05**; `geo` 0.33 replaced it with georust's `earcut` 0.4.x (MIT OR Apache-2.0, 9 unsafe lines for index newtypes, pinned `=0.4.5` by geo after a semver-incompatible 0.4 release broke builds).
- The one thing to know: triangulation of building footprints is available via `geo::TriangulateEarcut`; do not depend on either crate directly.

### cavalier_contours
- https://crates.io/crates/cavalier_contours, https://github.com/jbuckmccready/cavalier_contours; 0.9.0, 2026-08-20; MIT OR Apache-2.0; `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` in core (deps `ahash` with `no-rng`, `static_aabb2d_index`, `smallvec` carry a few unsafe lines); MSRV 1.88, edition 2024; wasm demo upstream and confirmed build here.
- Polylines with arc segments, parallel offsetting (open, closed, self-intersecting, with islands), booleans on closed non-self-intersecting polylines, round joins only, bulge limited to half circles.
- 0.6–0.9 each had breaking API changes and real robustness fixes (invalid offset slices, collapsed slices, repeat-position input, near-tangent merges).
- The one thing to know: it is the better pure-float offsetter for street outlines with rounded corners, but it is pre-1.0 with a breaking release every few months and only round joins; `i_overlay`'s buffer (via `geo::Buffer`) covers mitred/bevelled offsets on the integer grid.

### Straight-skeleton and offset crates
- `straight-skeleton` 0.2.1 (2026-07-17) is **GPL-2.0-or-later**: cannot be used (ADR-012).
- `geo-buffer` 0.2.0 (2023-06, Apache-2.0) straight-skeleton buffering, described upstream as presumably abandoned; `geo-buf` 0.1.0 (2025-01, Apache-2.0) is a fork adding mitre/round joins, 12 k downloads; `geo-offset` 0.4.0 (2025-02, ISC) is a port of the JS polygon-offset library, not a skeleton. Activity, unsafe and wasm status of all three unverified.
- The one thing to know: there is no maintained permissive straight-skeleton crate. The parcel-subdivision decision (milestone 6) should assume the skeleton is implemented in this repo or that recursive split wins.

### geo-booleanop
- https://crates.io/crates/geo-booleanop; 0.3.2, 2020-06-27; MIT; last push 2023-10-16; Martinez-Rueda port on geo 0.1x. Dead; `geo::BooleanOps` on `i_overlay` replaced it. Reject.

### noise and fastnoise-lite
- `noise` 0.9.0 (2024-03-23, Apache-2.0/MIT, no unsafe found, no_std feature) still depends on `rand 0.8`/`rand_xorshift 0.3`, which drags `rand_core 0.6` into the tree; repo pushed 2025-02-14, 49 open.
- `fastnoise-lite` 1.1.1 (2024-03-05, MIT, no unsafe found, `libm` feature for no_std); the Rust port lives in `Rust/` of the C++ repo and was last touched 2024-05-06.
- The one thing to know: both are fine for terrain-ish fields, but neither is hash-addressable by object id. Given ADR-004, a small value-noise or simplex over the project's own `splitmix64` hash avoids a dependency and a second RNG lineage. If one is used, `fastnoise-lite` is simpler and seedable per call.

### indexmap
- https://crates.io/crates/indexmap; 2.14.2, 2026-09-05; Apache-2.0 OR MIT; MSRV 1.85; deps `equivalent`, `hashbrown 0.17`; 12 unsafe lines plus hashbrown's 450.
- The one thing to know: iteration order is insertion order regardless of hasher, so the default `RandomState` does not leak randomness into output as long as nothing sorts by hash. Still, `BTreeMap` is the simpler story for the determinism lint.

### xxhash-rust
- https://crates.io/crates/xxhash-rust; 0.8.19, 2026-09-28; **BSL-1.0** (Boost, OSI-approved, permissive, no attribution in binaries); no runtime deps; no_std; 31 unsafe lines for SIMD.
- The one thing to know: ADR-018 already fixes `splitmix64` for seeding, so xxhash is only needed if a 64-bit hash of strings (object ids) is wanted; `xxh3_64` is stable across versions and platforms. Add `BSL-1.0` to the cargo-deny allow list if adopted.

## Recommended set per open decision

### Geometry crates (milestone 0 spike)

Recommendation: `geo-types` 0.7 for types, `spade` 2.15 for CDT/Voronoi, `geo` 0.33 for algorithms including `BooleanOps`, `Buffer`, hulls, simplification and earcut, with `rstar` held at 0.12 via `geo-types`'s `rstar_0_12` feature. Do not add `i_overlay` or `rstar 0.13` as direct dependencies until `geo` moves (rstar 0.13 is in geo's unreleased changelog); Jeffrey decides when that restriction is lifted, by ADR. Pin `libm =0.2.16`, `rand_pcg =0.10.2`.

Why: every crate in the set is MIT OR Apache-2.0, builds for wasm32 (verified), and the two crates on the determinism path are `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`: `spade`, which decides topology with exact predicates via `robust`, and `rstar`, which is an R*-tree spatial index and decides nothing. `i_overlay`'s integer snapping is a determinism feature, not a bug. The spike should exercise: duplicate and collinear road vertices through `try_bulk_load_cdt`, crossing constraints through `add_constraint_and_split`, zero-area and self-touching polygons through `BooleanOps`, and buffering of a street network with holes.

Rejected: `delaunator` (no constraints, no Voronoi); `geo-booleanop` (dead since 2020, superseded); `earcutr` (archived, replaced by `earcut` inside geo); `straight-skeleton` (GPL-2.0); `geo-buffer`/`geo-buf`/`geo-offset` (abandoned or unverified, tiny user base); `cavalier_contours` kept as the named fallback if `i_overlay` buffering of street outlines proves too coarse on the integer grid, at the price of pre-1.0 churn and round joins only; direct `i_overlay 9.x` rejected for now only because it would duplicate the 4.5 copy inside geo.

### YAML crate (milestone 1)

Recommendation: `serde-saphyr` 2.0, with `serde_path_to_error` wrapped around it for field paths in errors, `schemars` 1.2 for the published schema.

Why: it is the only candidate that is simultaneously maintained (release this month), `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` through its parser, verified on wasm32, serialising as well as deserialising (needed for `--write-back`, ADR-005), and strict about duplicate keys by default. The archived `serde_yaml` lineage (`serde_yaml_ng`, `serde_norway`) sits on a C-transliterated backend that RustSec marked unmaintained on 2026-10-12.

Rejected: `serde_yaml_ng` (unmaintained backend, last release 2024-05); `serde_norway` (same design, last release 2024-12; acceptable emergency fallback because it is API-identical to `serde_yaml`); `serde_yml` (archived, RUSTSEC-2025-0068); `noyalib` (0.0.x with 58 releases, same maintainer as `serde_yml`; re-evaluate in 2027); `serde_yaml_bw` (superseded by saphyr from the same author).

Risk to record in the ADR: saphyr is a single-maintainer crate and 2.0.0 is days old. Mitigation: the spec loader is one module behind a `load_spec(&str) -> Result<Spec>` function, so swapping the backend touches one file.

### Font metrics crate (milestone 7, Jeffrey decides)

Recommendation: `skrifa` for label measurement in core, because `resvg`/`usvg` 0.48 shape text with `skrifa` + `harfrust`; one font stack removes the font-parsing half of any width mismatch between core and the PNG; the other half is shaping, which `resvg` does with `harfrust` and core does not, so widths match only for text where shaping changes nothing (plain Latin without ligatures or kerning), and a tolerance is needed otherwise, and `skrifa` is `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`, no_std-capable and actively developed by Google Fonts. Bundle one or two OFL fonts as `include_bytes!` (choice of faces is Jeffrey's; OFL is compatible with MIT/Apache distribution of the binary, the font files keep their own license).

Conservative alternative, equally acceptable: `ttf-parser` 0.25 (forbid unsafe, zero allocation, MSRV 1.63, 5 open issues). It is in declared maintenance mode, so it is stable rather than dead; its advance-width sums ignore shaping and may drift by an unmeasured amount from resvg's harfrust output, which can change label placement and collision results even when a label fits inside its own box.

Rejected: `fontdue` (adds a rasteriser and 30 unsafe lines for nothing the pipeline needs; resvg already rasterises); shaping engines (`rustybuzz`, `harfrust`) in core (out of scope until non-Latin labels are needed).

Unverified before the ADR: `skrifa` on wasm32 (not in the scratch build; it declares no_std, which is not a build result), and `skrifa`'s 0.x breaking cadence.
